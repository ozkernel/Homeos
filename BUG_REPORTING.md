# Report a bug

Found something that isn't working? Before reporting it, check [Help and troubleshooting](SUPPORT.md) and [Installation](INSTALLATION.md).

## What to include

Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/ozkernel/homeos/issues/new) and include:

1. Your **HomeOS version**, from **Settings → Diagnostics**.
2. Your setup: Unraid, Docker Engine or Docker Desktop, and whether you use a reverse proxy.
3. What you expected, what actually happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
4. A screenshot or short log extract **only after removing private information**.

Please **do not** post passwords, API keys, cookies, setup tokens, `.env` files, private addresses or personal details.

## Security bugs

Do **not** open a public issue containing vulnerability details. Follow [Security reporting](SECURITY.md) instead.
