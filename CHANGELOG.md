# What's new

## v2.0.0 — 17 November 2026

- **Servers:** Unraid and Proxmox VE dashboards for infrastructure, storage, containers and virtual machines, with historical charts and alerts.
- **Network:** UniFi device and WAN activity, plus AdGuard Home DNS statistics.
- **Media:** Plex, Jellyfin, Seerr, Sonarr, Radarr, Prowlarr and qBittorrent integrations.
- **Workspace:** App links, notes, files, music, wiki pages, tasks, calendars and device records.
- **Personalisation:** Themes, icons, widgets, accounts, active sessions and notifications.
- **Other features:** FFmpeg-backed audio and media functions, account controls and Demo Mode with sample data.

**Docker image:** `ghcr.io/ozkernel/homeos:2.0.0` (Linux amd64; available after publication).
